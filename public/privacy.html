Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

Panda JWL-Sync is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All parsing, merging, and modification of your .jwlibrary backups happens entirely within your web browser using WebAssembly. Your personal study notes, highlights, and database files are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Google Drive Integration (Optional)

If you choose to use the optional Cloud Sync feature, you will authenticate directly with Google Drive. We use the restricted drive.file scope, which means the application can only access, create, or modify files within a specific "JW Sync" folder that it creates. It cannot read your other personal files on Google Drive.

Your authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

Cross-Device Sync & Checksum Index: To enable duplicate detection and notify you when new backups are uploaded from another device, a private metadata index file named .jwsync_index.json is kept inside your "JW Sync" folder. This file contains cryptographic SHA-256 checksums of your databases, upload timestamps, and anonymous device identifiers. This index stays strictly in your personal Google Drive and is never sent to any external server.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect any telemetry, usage analytics, or personal information. There is no backend server infrastructure recording your activity.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

The application uses your browser's Local Storage purely for maintaining your Google Drive connection state (if used) and persisting your local UI preferences (like theme or filter settings). No tracking cookies are used.

5. Disclaimer

6. Contact

As this is an open-source, client-side utility, all code can be inspected publicly. For any concerns regarding privacy or data handling, you may review the source code directly.
