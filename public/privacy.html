Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Processing

Panda JWL-Sync is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All parsing, merging, and modification of your .jwlibrary backups happens entirely within your web browser using WebAssembly. Your personal study notes, highlights, and database files are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Google Drive Integration (Optional)

If you choose to use the optional Cloud Sync feature, you will authenticate directly with Google Drive. We use the restricted drive.file scope, which means the application can only access, create, or modify files within a specific "JW Sync" folder that it creates. It cannot read your other personal files on Google Drive.

Your authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

Cross-Device Sync & Checksum Index: To enable automatic duplicate detection, instant checking of whether any loaded backup (in Merge, Stats, or Explorer) is already preserved in your cloud storage, and notifications when new backups are uploaded from another device, a private metadata index file named .jwsync_index.json is kept inside your "JW Sync" folder. This file contains cryptographic SHA-256 checksums of your databases, upload timestamps, and anonymous device identifiers. This index stays strictly in your personal Google Drive and is never sent to any external server.

3. Data Collection & Analytics

We do not collect any telemetry, usage analytics, or personal information. There is no backend server infrastructure recording your activity.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

The application uses your browser's Local Storage purely for maintaining your Google Drive connection state (if used) and persisting your local UI preferences (like theme or filter settings). No tracking cookies are used.

5. RGPD / GDPR Compliance (European Data Protection)

In full alignment with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation / RGPD):

  • Decentralized Processing: All processing is strictly local (edge processing). No personal data or user study notes are transmitted, hosted, or retained by Panda JWL-Sync operators.
  • Data Subject Rights (Articles 15–22 GDPR): Because no user data is stored on remote servers, you maintain complete sovereignty over your data at all times. You can access, export, or delete your data at any moment simply by managing your local files, clearing your browser storage, or deleting backups from your Google Drive.
  • Security of Processing (Article 32 GDPR): Optional client-side encryption (AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation) provides military-grade confidentiality for backups stored in the cloud. Encryption keys and passwords are never transmitted or stored remotely.

6. Contact & Support Form (Web3Forms)

When you contact us via the built-in support form, your message details (name, email address, message body, and optional diagnostic metadata) are securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS to our email dispatch provider, Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com), exclusively for delivering your inquiry to our support mailbox.

7-Day Retention Limit (GDPR Article 17): In accordance with Web3Forms security and GDPR policies, submitted form entries are temporarily retained on secure servers for a maximum period of 7 calendar days solely to ensure reliable email dispatch and delivery troubleshooting. After 7 days, form logs are automatically and irreversibly deleted. Your email address and message contents are never sold, shared, monetized, or used for marketing or profiling.

If you prefer not to use the web form, an option to reveal our direct support email address is provided on the contact page.

7. Disclaimer

Panda JWL-Sync has no affiliation, connection, endorsement, or official link with Jehovah's Witnesses, JW.org, JW Library, or any related organization.

8. Contact & Transparency

As this is an open-source, client-side utility, all source code is publicly auditable. For any concerns regarding privacy, security, or data handling, you may inspect the repository or open an issue directly.
